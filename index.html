          How it works

          Binaural beats

          Each ear gets a slightly different tone, for example 195 Hz left and 205 Hz right. Your brain perceives a gentle 10 Hz "beat" that isn't in the sound itself. This only works with headphones.

          Monaural beats

          The same two tones are mixed together before reaching your ears, so the beat is a real, physical wobble in loudness. It works on speakers too.

          Isochronic tones

          A single tone switched on and off at the beat rate. This is the most pronounced pulse of the three and it works on speakers.

          Noise & nature

          White, pink and brown noise or rain, waves and thunder mask distractions and make long sessions more pleasant. Brown noise and waves are the softest.

          Put your headphones on

          Binaural beats require stereo headphones or earbuds. Each ear must hear a different tone. Through speakers the two tones blend in the air and the effect is lost.

          • Start at a low volume and raise it gradually.
          • Do not use while driving, operating machinery, or doing anything that needs your full attention.
          • If you have epilepsy or a seizure disorder, a heart condition or pacemaker, or are pregnant, talk to a doctor first.
          • Stop if you feel discomfort, dizziness or anxiety.
          • This is not a medical device. Effects vary from person to person.

          Tip: press L/R next to the play button to check that your headphones are on the right way round.

          Shortcuts: Space play/pause · Esc stop.
