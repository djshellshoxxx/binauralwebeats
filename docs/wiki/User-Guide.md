# User Guide

Use headphones. Binaural beats need each ear to hear its own tone. The app asks you to confirm headphones before it plays, and the L/R button checks the channels.

1. Pick a frequency preset, a guided program, or set the beat and carrier manually.
2. Adjust the mixer: beats, noise, nature sounds, the Shepard illusion and master.
3. Optionally enable the 8D sweep, the pulse effect, or the breathing pacer.
4. Press play. Space plays or pauses and Esc stops.
5. Use the program editor for your own programs, or download a session as WAV (up to 30 minutes).

Health note: these tones are not medical treatment. The app shows a safety notice on first run.
